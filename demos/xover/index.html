    ABOUT THE DEMO ICDE 2027 · Research Track

    Multilingual Text-to-OverpassQL.
    Cross-lingual retrieval & execution voting.

    OSM XOver translates natural-language questions across eight languages into executable OverpassQL using cross-lingual example selection, multi-candidate execution voting, and conservative syntax repair.

    1 · C2 Selectml_8x + Profile-DPP
    →
    2 · Decode3 candidate queries
    →
    3 · C3 & C4Execution vote & repair

    K-shot & candidate budget

    Choose 0–12 English demonstrations (default 6; 0 runs zero-shot). The public demo executes 3 candidates (research benchmarks use 8) and selects via non-empty execution voting.

    Provenance & conservative C4

    Results execute against the project’s dated OpenStreetMap snapshot. Valid empty results are never rewritten; C4 applies output hygiene and at most one bounded syntax repair on parser errors.

    Historical selections and generated queries are labeled in Research details when reused. Independent academic research project using OpenStreetMap data; not affiliated with the OpenStreetMap Foundation.
