      ABOUT THE DEMO
      SIGMOD 2027 · Research Track
    
    
    
    Token-Aware Context Optimization.
    Ask, inspect, and verify on the map.
    
    
    OSM TACO translates natural-language geographic questions into executable OverpassQL while optimizing which in-context examples fit inside your token budget.
    
    
    
      
    1 · AskQuestion + token budget
    
      →
      
    2 · OptimizeInfluence + diversity ICL
    
      →
      
    3 · InspectOverpassQL & OSM map
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Interactive preview
    
        
    Loads three compositional showcase queries paired with dated OpenStreetMap snapshots (2022-07-04) so you can inspect results offline without calling an encoder or LLM.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Live pipeline
    
        
    Embeds your question with Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B and the trained AnglE-LoRA influence checkpoint, runs canonical taco_C_plus over the 6,180-example pool under the historical DeepSeek token budget, generates OverpassQL with GPT-6 Luna, and executes against dual project Overpass instances.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    Cache hits and historical model usage are explicitly labeled. Independent academic research project using OpenStreetMap data; not affiliated with the OpenStreetMap Foundation.
